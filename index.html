Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built from the ground up for real riders. Clean, fast, and reliable when it matters most.



📍
Real-time Location Sharing
See every member of your group live on the map. Location updates every 3 seconds with 5 metre accuracy — so no one gets left behind, ever.
🔗
Room System
Create a room in seconds, share a simple code. Anyone can join instantly — no contact sharing needed.
🚨
SOS Safety Alert
One tap sends an emergency alert to everyone in your group. Because safety is not optional.
🌙
Dark Mode
Easy on the eyes during night rides. Your theme preference is saved and remembered automatically.
🗺️
OpenStreetMap
Full English map, no API key, no hidden costs. Works everywhere in the world for free.
